Welcome

One day to dare to build

Techno Hacks is a student-led hackathon at DTU, started by three Technos: Rui, Larissa and Ingrid.

Team up with new people, build a prototype and pitch it, all in one day. Test an idea, try something new, or take a potential co-founder for a spin.

Engineer, designer, business mind or none of the above: you belong here.

What's “hacking”?

Not breaking into computers. Hacking means solving a problem creatively, with whatever you've got.

What's a hackathon?

Teams build something real, fast, and show it off at the end. Expect a few “wait, that actually works?” moments.

Questions

FAQ

I don't have a team or an idea

Most people don't. Come solo and join an idea at 11:00 to form a team of 3–5. You can't build solo, so if you're stuck, find a facilitator and we'll help.

Can I come with a team I already have?

Yes, as long as you're 3–5 people. You can also use the day to try building with someone new.

I can't code

You don't need to. Atech and Lovable work by describing what you want, and teams need designers and business minds just as much.

Why do I need a payment card?

Lovable and Mistral ask for a card when you redeem the event credits. Nothing is charged.

  • Lovable: the code covers your Pro month. Not keeping it? Cancel in Settings → Plans & Credits right away. Your 100 credits stay.
  • Mistral: you build on the €30 event credits. When you're done, cancel or remove your card on the Billing or Subscription page.
  • Atech: no card needed.

No credit card? A debit or virtual card usually works, or a teammate can redeem the credits.

How is judging done?

There's one challenge: Dare to build. Every team pitches to the jury and is scored on four things, equally:

  • Problem: is it real, for whom, and how do you know?
  • Build: how much of your idea can we see working?
  • Daring: what leap did you take today?
  • Pitch: would we want to join or back you?

The jury picks the Techno Hacks #1 winner. Built with Atech hardware? The Atech team also picks its best build, and that team keeps what it built.

Simulating part of your demo is fine, just say what's simulated. Keep screenshots in case the live demo breaks.

What can we win?

It's not about the prize. Winners walk away with something to build on: the best Atech build keeps what it built, and the Techno Hacks #1 winner gets something to keep going with, revealed on the day. We may also give an honourable mention for entrepreneurial spirit.

And everyone takes home the real win: a prototype, Lovable and Mistral credits to keep building, new teammates, maybe a co-founder, and new skills and inspiration for the next step.

What's there to eat?

Coffee, tea, fruit and maybe pastries from Madboks. Pizza slices for lunch from Atech, Casca sodas and water. All free. Big appetite? Bring a snack or your own lunch.

Will there be photos?

Yes, we'll take photos during the day. Prefer not to be in them? Just tell us at check-in.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes: DTU Guest (connect, open a browser and accept). DTU students can use eduroam.

I can't come anymore

Please cancel your spot on Luma so someone on the waitlist can take it.

Do I need to return the Atech kit?

Yes, please hand it back at the end of the day, unless your team wins Atech's best build. Then it's yours to keep.
